Situated in Vancouver and Burnaby, the Marianne and Edward Gibson Art Museum (the Gibson) occupies the unceded territories of xʷməθkwəy̓əm, Skwxwú7mesh, Səl̓ílwətaɬ, and kʷikʷəƛ̓əm Nations. Acknowledging that the activities of the Gibson unfold on colonized Indigenous territories is both a recognition that settler colonialism is an ongoing structure and a commitment to support the capacity of art to unsettle these conditions.

"SFU Galleries" is the organizational unit at Simon Fraser University which manages the Gibson.
